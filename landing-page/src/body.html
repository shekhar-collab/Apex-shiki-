FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need experience to join?+
Not at all. Every membership includes an onboarding assessment so your coach can tailor a program to your current level.
Can I pause or cancel my membership?+
Yes — all plans are flexible, with pausing available for travel or injury and no long-term lock-in required.
Is nutrition coaching included?+
Elite and Private plans include a custom nutrition plan built by our in-house nutrition team.
What are your operating hours?+
Our facility is open 5AM–11PM on weekdays and 6AM–9PM on weekends, with 24/7 access for Private members.
